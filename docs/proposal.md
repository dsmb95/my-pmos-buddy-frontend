## My PMOS Buddy

# Project Idea:
    - An app to keep track of all things PMOS (formerly known as PCOS). It assists you with tracking your flow, skin, weight, and medications/supplements. This is an all around tracker to help you figure out patterns or routines that works or not work for you. 
 
# Features:
    1. Track your flow:
        - The most common symptom of PMOS are irregular periods.
        - Using a calendar API (React-Calendar) and a Menstrual Cycle Prediction API, will provide cycle predictions and other helpful data.
    2. Track skin care
        - Upload pictures to see skin progress with current skin care routine.
        - Record current routine, and keep track of progress by providing notes.
    3. Track medicaitons / supplements.
        - Keep track of medicaitons/ supplements currently being taken.
        - Log medication name, dosage, and frequency.
        - Helps the use keep track of past medications taken and how well they responded to treatments. 
    4. Track weight
        - Log current weight by day, to keep track of weight fluctuations. 

# Target Audience:
    - This app is for all women especially those affected by PMOS. It is estimated that 10 - 15% of woemen are affected by this condition worldwide, which makes about 1 in every 8 women or a whoppping 170 million women globally. Despite it being common, it is reported that 70% of affected women remain undiagnosed. Thisis because of a very wide variety of symptoms like irreg periods, acne, weight gain etc.
