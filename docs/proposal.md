## My PMOS Buddy

# Project Idea:
    - An app to keep track of all things PMOS (formerly known as PCOS). It assists you with tracking your flow, skin, weight, and medications/supplements. This is an all around tracker to help you figure out patterns or routines that works or not work for you. 
 
# Features:
    1. Track your flow:
        - The most common symptom of PMOS are irregular periods.
        - Using a calendar API (React-Calendar) and a Menstrual Cycle Prediction API, will provide cycle predictions and other helpful data.
    2. Track skin care:
        - Log morning (AM) and night (PM) skincare routines and update them as needed.
        - Track daily skin symptoms (e.g., Acne, Dry, Oily, Itchy) and add detailed notes.
        - Upload multiple photos to visually track skin progress over time.
    3. Track medications / supplements:
        - Keep track of medications/supplements currently being taken.
        - Log medication name, dosage, and frequency.
        - Helps the user keep track of past medications taken and how well they responded to treatments. 
    4. Track weight:
        - Log weight entries over time with specific units (lbs or kg).
        - View a visual chart of weight fluctuations to easily spot trends.

# Target Audience:
    - This app is for all women especially those affected by PMOS. It is estimated that 10 - 15% of women are affected by this condition worldwide, which makes about 1 in every 8 women or a whoppping 170 million women globally. Despite it being common, it is reported that 70% of affected women remain undiagnosed. This is because of a very wide variety of symptoms like irregular periods, acne, weight gain etc. (World Health Organization).
